03 / How to play

From first message to photo card.

Step 01

Choose a character

Find a story on the Explore screen, read the opening scene, and send your first message.

Step 02

Play through the chapters

Reply in chat and make choices to move the story forward and level up with your character.

Step 03

Build your collection

Unlock photo cards as you level up and discover themed Secret Albums along the way.

Daily check-ins award free gems, with a bonus on Day 7. The game is available in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Indonesian, and Arabic.
